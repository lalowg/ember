    The fire Chef Isla Varga

    One hearth, lit every morning, cooks everything we serve.

    Isla Varga spent fifteen years in city kitchens before taking over a disused foundry and building a hearth where the furnace used to stand. There is no gas line at EMBER. The day is shaped by the fire, and the menu follows what the fire is doing.
